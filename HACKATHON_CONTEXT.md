## 4. Idea Selection Criteria (Internal Strategy)
To maximize score in **Round 1 (Activity 01 → 02 → 03)** and stand out from common clichés (avoiding the prompt's exact examples like electricity bills, clinic booking, or generic Kirana bookkeeping):
1. **Common-India Daily Pain:** Affects ordinary Indians daily or multiple times a week.
2. **Non-Negotiable Voice:** Typing/tapping genuinely fails in the real-world moment (hands busy, eyes busy, moving/commuting, panic/urgency, or regional code-mixed speech vs. English keyboards).
3. **Clean Prototype Scope:** Easily expressed as an end-to-end AI app-builder prompt in Activity 02.
